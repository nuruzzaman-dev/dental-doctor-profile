# Morgan Dental Studio — Refactor Direction

## Ground truth
The supplied references establish the target direction: modern dental brand websites with strong hero communication, high-contrast sans-serif typography, fresh clinic colors, real doctor and treatment imagery, practical contact information near the top, full-width section blocks, visible CTAs, and open image-led service presentation. The third supplied Dentello URL currently returns 404 and is not used as a visual source until a working URL is provided.

## Chosen design movement
Contemporary healthcare brand design with a Webflow-style marketing rhythm: confident, warm, energetic, and conversion-aware. The experience should feel like a real clinic brand with a point of view, not an editorial template or component-library demo.

## Core principles
The page uses full-bleed color fields instead of repeated boxed cards. Typography is heavy, clean, and readable, with large sans-serif headlines and short supporting copy. Contact, hours, location, and booking are treated as high-priority trust content. Motion should reveal relationships between image and text, not decorate every component.

## Visual system
The new palette is deep navy, mineral teal, pale mint, airy sky blue, and warm sand. Teal owns the brand and CTA moments; navy carries credibility and contrast; mint and sky create a clean clinical atmosphere without using generic hospital blue. Typography uses Manrope for display and DM Sans for supporting copy.

## Layout and interaction
The hero is a practical split composition with one emotional image and one direct promise. A contact band follows immediately. The doctor story is a full-image plus text section with visible credentials and measurable trust signals. Services are an open numbered list with hover movement, not cards. The care approach uses a full-width image field and stacked principles. Questions are disclosed with lightweight accordion behavior. Booking remains available in the mobile bottom bar.

## Content rules
Doctor information stays specific: BDS, MSc Restorative Dentistry, focus areas, education, clinic location, hours, and phone contact. No fabricated testimonials, ratings, or patient reviews will be added. Patient concerns are represented through transparent FAQ content instead.
